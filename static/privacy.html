Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy notice explains, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the nature, scope and purpose of processing personal data when using "BetaCodex Chat" ("this website" or "the service"). This English text is a courtesy translation; the German version is the legally binding version for German data-protection purposes.

In brief: BetaCodex Chat is an AI knowledge assistant that answers questions about the BetaCodex based on a curated source collection - with a source citation for every statement. Unlike other AI assistants, these sources are curated for genuine decentralization, functioning self-organization, and democratic organizational design. An additional Creative Mode helps you draft your own texts about the BetaCodex.

The service is operated by Beta Hof GmbH and hosted on a server in Germany (Hetzner Cloud, Falkenstein).

For visitors, there is no analytics or advertising tracking, and no user account is required. For internal quality assurance, we permanently store a small excerpt of questions, not linked to an IP address or account (the first question of each new conversation, questions the knowledge assistant could not answer, and questions you give feedback on - see section 5); this data cannot be attributed to any person. Data is only attributed to a person for invited editors who sign in by email (section 8). See the following sections for full details.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing under the GDPR is the entity named in this website's legal notice (Impressum). Full contact details (name, address, email) are provided there.

2. General information and your rights

We process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and our services, based on your consent or another legally permitted basis under Art. 6(1) GDPR. Your rights as a data subject are summarised in section 9.

3. Providing the website and server log files

When you access this website, our server automatically collects technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time of the request, the page/resource requested, the amount of data transferred, browser type and version, and operating system. This data is used solely to ensure reliable and secure operation (e.g. abuse detection, troubleshooting) and is not combined with other data sources. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operational security).

4. Cookies (sign-in)

This website uses a single technically necessary cookie (session cookie), set only when an editor signs in via the magic-link login. It keeps you signed in during your visit, is cryptographically signed against tampering, not readable by client-side JavaScript ("HttpOnly"), and expires automatically. No tracking, behavioural analytics, or advertising cookies are used, including none by third parties. Visitors who only ask questions without signing in receive no cookie at all. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the usage relationship) or (f) (legitimate interest in secure authentication).

5. AI-assisted question answering

This website's core purpose is answering questions based on curated source material. When you ask a question, it is transmitted together with the matching excerpts from our source collection to Anthropic PBC (San Francisco, USA) to generate the answer (the "Claude" language model). As Anthropic is based in the US, this involves a transfer to a third country, which relies on Anthropic's Standard Contractual Clauses or a comparable adequacy mechanism. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing this website's core function).

Beyond the processing described above, which is used only to generate the answer, we additionally store permanently (not linked to your IP address or any account, but including the question and answer text and a timestamp): the first question of each new conversation, any question the source material could not answer or could only partially answer according to the system instructions, and any question for which you give feedback via the thumbs-up/-down icons. This data is used solely for internal analysis of topic gaps and answer quality and is only accessible to system administrators in the internal question log; none of it is recorded when system administrators use the service themselves, or in the development environment. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in quality assurance and improving the service).

6. Voice input and output

Voice input (microphone): Speech-to-text conversion happens entirely locally in your browser via its built-in speech recognition. No audio data is sent to our server or any third party.

Voice output (read-aloud): If you use the feature to have an answer read aloud, the answer text is transmitted to Google Ireland Limited (part of Google/Alphabet, USA) for speech synthesis (Google Cloud Text-to-Speech). This only happens when you actively use this optional feature. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through active use of the feature).

7. Abuse protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To protect the question feature and contact form from automated abuse (e.g. bots causing unnecessary cost), we use the "Turnstile" service by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). This transmits technical information about your device/browser and your IP address to Cloudflare to distinguish automated access from human use. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in abuse prevention).

8. Editor sign-in (email magic link)

Access to editorial functions (creating/editing sources) is restricted to invited individuals. Signing in sends a one-time login link to the email address you provide. Your email address, optionally provided name, the email address of the person who invited you, and the time of your most recent sign-in are stored for as long as your access exists, solely for authentication and to track editorial changes (audit log). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

9. Processing of uploaded content (editors only)

When invited editors upload audio files or PDFs to be added to the source collection, these are transmitted to OpenAI, L.L.C. (USA) for automatic transcription/text recognition, and optionally to Anthropic for automatic summarisation (see section 5). This applies only to content deliberately imported by editors, not to regular visitors' data.

10. Recipients and processors

Your data is only transmitted to the service providers named above, acting as processors or under their own responsibility as applicable. We do not sell your data. The required safeguards under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR (in particular EU Standard Contractual Clauses or participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where offered by the respective provider) are in place for the US-based providers used (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare).

11. Retention period

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for its purpose: server log files are deleted or anonymised after a short period; editor account data is retained until access is revoked or deletion is requested. The question/answer excerpts described in section 5 (first question per conversation, unanswered questions, questions with feedback) are currently not subject to any automatic deletion period and are retained until manually deleted by a system administrator; all other question text is not stored beyond generating the answer. Statutory retention obligations remain unaffected.

12. Your rights as a data subject

You have the right to access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with future effect. Please contact the address given in the legal notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

13. Data security

This website is served exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Access to editorial functions is technically restricted via a role model. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against accidental or intentional manipulation, loss, destruction, or unauthorised access.

14. Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy whenever data processing or applicable law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
